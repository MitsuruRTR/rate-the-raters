Rate The "raters"

Rating the raters.

Many retail traders rely on broker-review sites as their touchstone for judging the trustworthiness and safety of FX brokers. Yet those review sites all too often abandon the very role they claim to serve — taking money from brokers in exchange for high scores, or demanding payment on the threat of a low one. When the "rating" itself, the thing you're supposed to be able to trust, can't be trusted, what is a retail trader supposed to believe?

Traders around the world need a place where they can compare review sites with confidence. That's why this site exists. We do not rate any FX broker itself. And here, scores are not for sale — that's the rule. Our subject is strictly the raters — the review sites themselves — and we want you to judge their trustworthiness for yourself, here. This is a site for retail traders everywhere.

    About Our Methodology

    We score each of 6 axes out of 10 points, for a total of 60 points. Every score is based solely on publicly available information — we never rely on non-public information or speculation.

    Excellent45–60 pts
    Good37–44 pts
    Some Concerns25–36 pts
    Caution13–24 pts
    Not Recommended0–12 pts
    01

    Transparency of Operator Information

    Are the operating company, address, representative, and contact details clearly stated?

    02

    Disclosure of Rating Logic

    Is the method used to calculate scores disclosed, or is it a black box?

    03

    Disclosure of Revenue Model

    Are relationships with advertisers and partners disclosed? Is there independence between listing fees and ratings?

    04

    Review Authenticity Safeguards

    Is there identity verification for reviewers, and are there safeguards against impersonation or duplicate submissions?

    05

    Consistency with Regulatory Data

    How well does the site's information line up with official licensing data from bodies such as the FSA, FCA, or CySEC?

    06

    History of Problem Cases

    Is there a public record of corrective orders, lawsuits, or accusations of demands for payment?

    Our Comment Moderation Policy

    Each case page also accepts comments and firsthand accounts from users. However, letting "anyone post anything" is a textbook way for a review site to undermine its own credibility — precisely the problem this site exists to audit in others — so we operate under the following policy instead.

    01

    Pre-publication review

    Submissions are not published instantly; our team reviews the content first.

    02

    No effect on the official score

    Comments are published purely as reference information and have absolutely no effect on the official 6-axis score. Conflating the two would let anyone manipulate a score simply by flooding a page with posts.

    03

    Verification status always shown

    Every comment is permanently labeled "unverified (personal account)." Only comments with supporting evidence (such as a URL) are marked differently.

    04

    Removal criteria published in advance

    We never remove a comment in exchange for payment. We remove only content we can confirm is factually incorrect, contains personal information, or constitutes harassment.

    05

    A reporting function

    Other readers can flag a suspicious comment through a dedicated reporting channel.

    06

    Posting-frequency limits

    We limit rapid, repeated posting from the same user in a short time window to prevent coordinated manipulation campaigns.
